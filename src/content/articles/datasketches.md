A dashboard compresses the world into a number. A datasketch runs that pipeline backwards: it takes a measurement and opens it back up into something you can see and hear.

## How a sketch is made

The input can be a song, a time series or anything else that changes over time. It is measured frame by frame (loudness, frequency bands, onsets, trends), and each of those values is wired to something visual: the colour of a field, the speed of a camera, the brightness of a light. The pieces are built in Blender and TouchDesigner and rendered as finished clips.

## Where they live

Finished sketches are published on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@hyperadapted), and stills and loops from the work in progress go to [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/hyperadapted/).

Hyperadapted is where the creative work comes together. Essays, audiovisual sketches and music used to be scattered across a handful of platforms, each with its own feed and its own idea of what a post should look like. This site is the one place that holds all of it.

## What you'll find

- **Writing.** Longer pieces on perception, data and making things. Some are published here, others on Medium or Substack, and the Articles page links to each one wherever it lives.
- **Audiovisual.** Datasketches: short pieces where a measurement drives the picture. The finished ones go up on YouTube, the work in progress on Instagram.
- **Music.** Tracks and sound pieces, usually released with the visuals they drive.

## Why a hub

Every platform shapes what gets made for it. A hub that belongs to no platform keeps the work in one line, in the order it was made, so it can be read as one body of work rather than four feeds.
